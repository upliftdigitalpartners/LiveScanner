# Installing Live Scanner

Live Scanner isn't on the Play Store, so you install it straight from the app file (`app-release.apk`).
You need an **Android phone running 8.0 or newer**. (No iPhone version yet.)

## 1. Install on your phone
1. Download **`app-release.apk`** (from the link you were sent).
2. Tap the file. Android will warn that it can't install from this source — tap **Settings**, turn on
   **“Allow from this source”** for whatever app opened it (Files, Chrome, etc.), go back, and tap **Install**.
3. Open **Live Scanner**. Air-traffic-control feeds play right away — tap one. Use **Nearby** to find
   feeds close to you, and the ☆ to save favorites.

## 2. Listen in the car (Android Auto) — one-time setup
Sideloaded apps stay hidden in Android Auto until you allow them. On your phone:

1. Open **Android Auto settings** (Settings → Apps → Android Auto → open its settings).
2. Scroll to **“Version”** and tap it about **10 times** until developer mode turns on.
3. Open the **⋮ menu → Developer settings** and switch on **“Unknown sources.”**
4. Go back, open **“Customize launcher,”** and tick **Live Scanner**.
5. Plug your phone into the car — Live Scanner now shows up with your other audio apps.

*(Wording varies slightly by Android Auto version, but the switch you need is always
**Developer settings → Unknown sources**.)*

## 3. Updates
When you get a newer `app-release.apk`, just install it over the old one — same steps, your favorites stay.

## Notes
- **Scanner (police/fire/EMS) feeds** aren't bundled. Add them with **＋ Add feed**: paste a direct
  stream URL, or a Broadcastify feed ID (reliable playback for Broadcastify needs a Premium account,
  entered under **Settings**). Air-traffic feeds need no account.
- Listening to air-traffic control is legal in the US. **Mobile use of police scanners is restricted
  in some states** — check your local laws before using scanner feeds while driving.
